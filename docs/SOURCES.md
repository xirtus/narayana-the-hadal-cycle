# Natural history and asset provenance

All animal meshes, terrain, vegetation, materials, particles, interface icons and sound effects were created procedurally for this project. No Sketchfab, stock photography, extracted game assets or externally licensed animal models are included.

Typography requests Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans from Google Fonts; system serif/sans-serif fallbacks remain available when offline. Three.js and Vite are MIT-licensed; dependency license files remain in their installed packages.

References consulted on 6 September 2026:

- [Monterey Bay Aquarium: squid season](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/about-us/stories/squid-fishing-monterey-bay): California market squid in Monterey Bay.
- [Monterey Bay Aquarium: giant Pacific octopus](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/visit/exhibits/giant-pacific-octopus): eight-arm anatomy, suckers and camouflage.
- [Monterey Bay Aquarium: kelp forest](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/visit/exhibits/kelp-forest): the regional starting habitat.

- [MBARI: barreleye fish](https://www.mbari.org/animal/barreleye-fish/): transparent head and tubular eyes.
- [MBARI: vampire squid](https://www.mbari.org/animal/vampire-squid/) and [Monterey Bay Aquarium: vampire squid](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/animals-the-ocean/animals-a-to-z/vampire-squid): scavenging behavior and feeding filaments.
- [MBARI: deep-sea bioluminescence](https://www.mbari.org/news/new-study-shows-that-three-quarters-of-deep-sea-animals-make-their-own-light/): importance of biological light in deep habitats.
- [Monterey Bay Aquarium: common market squid](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/animals-the-ocean/animals-a-to-z/common-market-squid): feeding tentacles, arms and beak anatomy.
- [NOAA: light in the ocean](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/light_travel.html) and [light, colour and bioluminescence](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/lesson/bioluminescence-light-and-color/): sunlight zones and selective absorption of colours with depth.
- [NOAA: bioluminescence](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/ocean-fact/bioluminescence/): light generated by marine organisms.

The natural-light renderer removes red before blue and fades sunlight to zero by 1,000 metres. Spectral perception is a fictional visual translation of fluorescence and thermal signatures, not a claim that sunlight becomes ultraviolet or infrared in the deep ocean. Hunting reach, swimming speeds and growth are tuned for the playable divine animal; they are not measured performance claims for a real market squid.

Species ranges are broad documented habitat envelopes used for spawning, not claims that every species inhabits every point within a depth interval. Models and population densities are composed for a readable game. Supernatural pulses, divine cephalopod transformations and mythic species are fictional. The journal labels mythic beings separately.

Vedic motifs and the preservation/transformation story follow the supplied creative plan. They are an artistic interpretation, not a comprehensive account of Hindu belief.

The route combat uses fictional game behaviours: hunter patrols, shark attack telegraphs, stamina-based evasion, enhanced cephalopod strikes and damaging whale pressure waves. These are not natural-history claims about aggression or acoustic injury. Scuba models appear only in the surface region; deeper hunter vehicles are fictional submersibles. Whales and sharks beyond the plausible natural setting are explicitly marked as Asuric/mythic encounters.

The user's references to Maneater and [ABZÛ](https://abzugame.com/) inform the balance between responsive creature combat and quiet underwater exploration. Enemy models, control implementation, habitat restoration and interface artwork are original procedural work; no assets or code from those games are included.

## Expedition references — 9 September 2026

- [Atlantis: Imperial Depths](https://app.usecrayon.ai/play/82e970f7-0d86-421a-bdfc-5ac113b22bc0), supplied by the user, was inspected in a browser. Its terraced palace, circular colonnades, aqueducts and bridges informed the original procedural temple region. A local reference screenshot is recorded at `artifacts/atlantis-reference.png`; no game code, models or textures were extracted.
- [NOAA: Titanic bow](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/multimedia/daily-image-media-20210415/) gives the wreck's approximate depth as 3,800 metres. The game's requested 12,500 feet corresponds to 3,810 metres.
- [NOAA: The Titanic wrecksite](https://oceantoday.noaa.gov/fullmoon-titanicwrecksite/welcome.html) describes the rusticles that informed the wreck surface details. The game uses a compositional interpretation of bow, stern, boilers and scattered debris, not a measured reconstruction.

The user requested exploration ideas associated with Ecco the Dolphin and ABZÛ. The implementation adds original numbered sonar seals, dolphin-guided current courses, shoal rescues and contiguous space unlocks. Hollow-earth geography and the central black hole are explicitly mythic. The final spaces retain a compact underwater coordinate system suitable for play; their depth display does not model travel to the geological centre of Earth.
