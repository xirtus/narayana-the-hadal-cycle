# Drowned metropolis: optional bonus, rebuild later

The user rejected the current city visuals. Preserve this prototype and its rescue mission as an optional atlas destination. It must never be the mandatory opening or a prerequisite for the main descent.

A later pass needs a complete realistic AAA visual glow-up: convincing building proportions, identifiable metropolitan streets, varied architecture, authored damage and sediment, marine colonization, credible materials, strong compositions and environmental storytelling. More repetitions of the current blocks will not solve it.

The existing city lives in `src/expedition/SpaceGeometry.js`, selected by `space.id === "city"`. It is always accessible as a bonus from the atlas. Completing it returns the player to the natural reef route and does not unlock deeper regions.

Possible bonus lessons: crawling, arm anchors, ink, sonar, predator avoidance and human technology. Keep these optional. Work on the natural terraced ocean first.
